Mouse Firmware Upgrade User Manual
February 4, 2024

Firmware Upgrade Instructions:

1. ★ Upgrade Order:
First, upgrade the receiver firmware, then the mouse firmware. Be sure to connect the mouse with a USB cable during the upgrade. 
Ensure that both the mouse and receiver have sufficient battery and remain connected. If the wireless function does not work after upgrading, restart your computer and upgrade again. 
If you encounter any problems, please contact customer support at support@g-wolves.com.

2. ★ Receiver Firmware Upgrade Steps:

   1>  Open the receiver firmware upgrade tool.
   2>  Follow the instructions provided by the tool.
   3>  Upon successful upgrade, “PASS” will be displayed, then close the tool.
3. ★ Mouse Firmware Upgrade Steps:

   1>Ensure that the receiver firmware has been upgraded successfully, then connect the mouse with a USB cable and launch the mouse firmware upgrade tool.
   2>Follow the instructions provided by the tool.
   3>Upon successful upgrade, “PASS” will be displayed, then close the tool.
4. ★ Close all upgrade tools to complete the upgrade process.
