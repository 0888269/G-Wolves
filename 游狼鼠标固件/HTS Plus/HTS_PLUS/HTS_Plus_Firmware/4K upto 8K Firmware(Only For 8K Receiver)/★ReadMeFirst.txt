Update Steps:
★Please make sure your have 8K Receiver（Gray Transparent Shell）
1、Firstly, purchase the 8K receiver for HTS Plus from g-wolves.com.
2、Plug the receiver into the computer，run 'step1.8K_Receiver_Firmware'，Once you see "Pass", close the software and remove the receiver.
3、Next, connect the mouse to the computer using a data cable and run "step2.HTS_Plus_8K_Mouse_Firmware". Wait until you see "Pass", then close the software and proceed.
4、Connect the 8K receiver to the computer. Run "step3.pair_tools", you need to click pair buttons, just ensure that the upper and lower sets of labels turn green, indicating success. Then close the software and unplug the mouse cable to continue.
5、If you cannot run "step3.pair_tools",please run "step3.pair_tools-2".
6、Plug the receiver into the computer and switch on the mouse power at the bottom. After the wireless mouse can function properly, visit mouse.xyz or mouse.wiki to adjust mouse settings.
Following these six steps completes the update process, allowing you to use the mouse normally.



更新步骤：
★请确保你有8k接收器（灰色透明外壳）
1、首先，在g-wolves.com官网，购买HSK Plus的8K接收器。
2、把接收器链接电脑，运行"step1.8K_Receiver_Firmware"，等待看到“Pass”后，关闭此软件，并继续。
3、使用数据线把鼠标连接到电脑，运行"step2.HTS_Plus_8K_Mouse_Firmware”。等待看到“Pass”后，关闭此软件，并继续。
4、鼠标和鼠标接收器同时连接到电脑上，运行”step3.pair_tools”，你需要点击pair按钮，只需看到上下两组标签变成绿色，表示通过配对操作了。然后关闭此软件，拔掉鼠标电缆以继续。
5、如果"step3.pair_tools"无法使用，请使用"step3.pair_tools-2"
运行”step3.8k_receiver_Firmware”，等待看到“Pass”后，关闭此软件,并拔出接收器。
6、在鼠标底部打开鼠标电源开关。无线鼠标可以正常使用后，访问mouse.xyz或mouse.wiki，设置鼠标参数。
经过上述6个步骤之后，这样就完成了更新，你可以根据需要对鼠标进行正常使用了。
